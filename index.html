Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about custom neon ordering and delivery.

Pre-made signs are dispatched within 24 hours and take 2-3 working days. Custom text signs are handcrafted and take 3-5 working days for delivery nationwide.
Every MO Traders neon sign comes pre-drilled with mounting holes. We include free wall mounting screws, stainless steel spacers, or heavy-duty double-sided acrylic tape depending on your preference.
For pre-made signs, 100% Cash on Delivery is available. For personalized custom name signs, a small advance commitment deposit (e.g., 20-30%) via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or Bank Deposit is required, with the remaining balance collected at delivery.
We offer a 100% Transit Replacement Guarantee. If your sign arrives broken or damaged, simply record an unboxing video and send it to our WhatsApp helpline (+92 300 1234567) within 24 hours for a instant free replacement.
